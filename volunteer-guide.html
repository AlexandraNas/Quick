Good help, done safely.

Everything you need to know before your first task.

Before the task

  • Read the task details and only offer help you can give.
  • Keep all messages on QuickVolunteer.
  • Check the time and place, and let someone you trust know where you are going.

During the task

  • Be on time, friendly and respectful.
  • Never accept or ask for money, gifts or bank details.
  • Do not go inside someone's home unless the task needs it and you feel comfortable.

After the task

  • Confirm the task is completed so it is added to your activity history.
  • Leave an honest, kind review.
  • If anything worried you, report a concern. Our team reviews every report.
